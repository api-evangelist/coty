# Coty (coty)

Coty Inc. is one of the world's largest beauty companies with an iconic portfolio of brands across fragrance, color cosmetics, skin and body care, organized into Prestige and Consumer Beauty divisions. Coty does not publish a public developer API; supplier and partner integrations occur through the Coty Supplier Portal.
