NEW YORK – May 13, 2026 – Coty Inc. (NYSE: COTY) (Paris: COTY), (“Coty” or “the Company”) one of the world's largest beauty companies, today announced that its enterprise AI literacy program, "Supercharge with AI: Scaling Enterprise-Wide AI Literacy & Adoption to Transform How Coty Works," (Supercharge with AI), has been named a winner of Newsweek’s AI Impact Award in the “AI Workplace: Best Outcomes, Employee Engagement” category.
